RULES & SHORTCUTS

TRANSPARENT SCORING FORMULA

Every correct answer awards points using a formula designed to reward both precision and speed:

basePoints = 100
speedBonus = round(50 × remainingTime / allowedTime)
streakBonus = min(currentStreak × 5, 25)
totalQuestionScore = basePoints + speedBonus + streakBonus

Incorrect answers and timeouts earn 0 points. No negative penalties are applied.
